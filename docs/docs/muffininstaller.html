MuffinInstaller

Install MuffinEMU, keep it signed, and update it — all on your iPhone or iPad, with your own Apple ID. No computer. Latest: checking…

One tap from the web

Open the MuffinInstaller page in Safari on your iPhone or iPad and tap a certificate to install MuffinEMU. No computer.

Each one-tap card is a signed install of the latest MuffinEMU, and shows the certificate's name and how many days it has left. Tap Install MuffinEMU and confirm the prompt. Then trust the developer profile under Settings > General > VPN & Device Management.

If no cards are showing, no certificates have been added to the installer yet. Use your own Apple ID or certificate in the app instead, below.

Open the MuffinInstaller page →

Use your own certificate

On the installer page, the Use my own certificate card takes your .p12, its password, and your .mobileprovision. The page doesn't sign anything and doesn't upload your files. It tells you to load the same three files in MuffinEMU under Settings > Storage > MuffinInstaller > Use your own certificate, where they're signed on the device.

In the app: sign in with your Apple ID

  1. Get MuffinEMU on your device first (SideStore, AltStore or TrollStore; see the Installation page).
  2. Open MuffinEMU and go to Settings > Storage > MuffinInstaller.
  3. Connect LocalDevVPN.
  4. On iOS 27, tap Pair on this device. On iOS 17.4 to 26, import a pairing file made on a computer once.
  5. Choose Sign in with your Apple ID, Use your own certificate, or Install a signed IPA.
  6. MuffinInstaller signs and installs MuffinEMU. iOS closes the app as the new copy goes on; reopen it.
Your Apple ID is yours

MuffinInstaller signs with your account, on your device. Your password goes only to Apple's own sign-in, and a two-factor code goes only to Apple.

3. The dashboard

After MuffinInstaller has installed MuffinEMU, Settings > Storage > MuffinInstaller becomes a dashboard:

  • How this copy is signed, and the date the signature runs out.
  • Refresh now to re-sign before it expires, and Refresh automatically to do it on its own.
  • Update to the newest release, or choose a version.
  • The pairing file, the tunnel and JIT at a glance.
Only for a copy MuffinInstaller installed

Refresh and Update manage a MuffinEMU that MuffinInstaller signed. If you installed MuffinEMU another way (SideStore, TrollStore, a computer), sign in here and let MuffinInstaller install it once, and from then on it manages itself.

Which iOS version does what

iOS 27 or later

  • Everything, with no computer ever.
  • MuffinInstaller makes the pairing file on the device.
  • Sign, renew and update entirely on-device.

iOS 17.4 to 26

  • Import a pairing file made on a computer once.
  • After that, MuffinInstaller renews and updates on-device.
  • On-device pairing needs iOS 27.

Older than iOS 17.4

  • MuffinInstaller isn't available.
  • MuffinEMU still runs (iOS 15+); install and keep it signed with SideStore or AltStore from a computer.
