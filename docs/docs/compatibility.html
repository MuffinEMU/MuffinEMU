Game compatibility

Wii U games players have run in MuffinEMU on iPhone and iPad.

Reports come from players of MuffinEMU, and from players of EspressU, an app built on the same emulator code, in October 2026. Frame rates depend on the device, on JIT, on graphic packs and on the version.

What the statuses mean

  • Runs well: Reported at a steady 60 fps or close to it on at least one device.
  • Playable: Reported playable; frame rate or small problems vary.
  • Runs with issues: Starts and plays, with a clear problem such as audio or graphics.
  • Doesn't work yet: Reported not working.
  • Reported working: Reported working on at least one device, without more detail yet.

Report a game

Tell us what you played.

Say the game, your device, the iOS version, whether JIT was on, and how it ran. Post it in the MuffinEMU Discord (#game-tests) or open an issue. A game that isn't listed hasn't been reported yet, so it may boot, run slowly or not start.
