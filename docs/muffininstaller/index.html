How the one-tap install works
  1. Open this page on your iPhone or iPad in Safari.
  2. Pick a certificate marked valid and tap Install, then confirm the prompt.
  3. Wait for MuffinEMU to finish installing on your Home Screen.
  4. Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap the developer profile and Trust it.
  5. If a certificate is revoked or won't open, install with another — or use your own certificate below.

Use my own certificate

Have your own enterprise certificate? Upload the .p12, its password and the matching .mobileprovision. Everything stays in your browser and on your device — your key is never uploaded to this site or anywhere else.

Signing with your own key happens on your device, so the key stays private. We'll walk you through finishing in MuffinInstaller.

Keep it signed

Let MuffinInstaller renew MuffinEMU so it never expires.

  1. Open MuffinEMU and go to Settings › Storage › MuffinInstaller.
  2. Connect LocalDevVPN. On iOS 27 it pairs on-device; on 17.4–26 you import a pairing file once.
  3. Sign in with your Apple ID, or load your own certificate — the same one you can upload above.
  4. MuffinInstaller keeps MuffinEMU installed and up to date from then on, refreshing before a free Apple ID's 7 days run out.
