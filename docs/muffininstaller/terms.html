Using MuffinInstaller

The plain terms for installing MuffinEMU through this page or the in-app installer.

What this is

MuffinInstaller is the official installer for MuffinEMU, a free and open-source Wii U emulator for iPhone and iPad. It installs MuffinEMU in two ways: a one-tap web install from a signing certificate, and an in-app installer that keeps MuffinEMU signed with your own Apple ID or certificate.

Your Apple ID and your data

When you sign in with your own Apple ID, it is used only on your device to sign MuffinEMU, and it is sent only to Apple. It is never sent to this site, to MuffinEMU, or to anyone else. Pairing happens on-device on iOS 27, or once from an imported pairing file on iOS 17.4–26.

Certificates

Any one-tap install certificate listed here is added by its own owner, for their own use. No stolen, leaked or shared enterprise certificates are distributed. An install only begins when you tap it, and each one shows where the build comes from first.

Games and keys

No copyrighted content is distributed through MuffinInstaller or MuffinEMU — not games, not system files, not keys. Bring your own.

No warranty

MuffinInstaller and MuffinEMU are provided as-is, with no warranty. You install and use them at your own risk. The source is on GitHub.

Approach based on SideInstaller by FrizzleM — an independent, non-commercial re-implementation for MuffinEMU.
